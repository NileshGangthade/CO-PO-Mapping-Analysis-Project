Student Performance Assessment

A web-based system that automates the process of calculating CO-PO attainment for a particular subject. The system will allow faculty members to input CO and PO criteria, track student performance, and generate reports based on the evaluation results. This will enable faculty members to efficiently evaluate student performance and identify areas where improvements are needed.

Amazing Services & Features for you

The system will provide a user-friendly interface that will allow faculty members and administrators to access the data and generate reports to analyze the performance of students and the effectiveness of the teaching-learning process.
